Assumptions & sources

Planning notes so families know what is solid fact vs. estimated mapping. Confidence labels are honest on purpose.

Core assumptions

  • Published UC freshman GPA bands are honors-weighted capped. The explorer slider uses a 2.0–4.0 UC-weighted A–G planning scale.
  • Planning freshman mid-50 GPA bands are a conservative mapping (medium confidence).
  • TAG minimum GPA planning default is ~3.2 where a campus-specific minimum is uncertain (medium confidence).
  • CSU and private mid-50 / admit figures are approximate planning data (medium / low confidence).
  • Planning charge / cost figures stay out of the main UI; they may log to the browser console for counselor use only.
  • UC SAT/ACT policy for fall 2027: not required and not considered (per UC Academic Senate review, July 2026 news cycle). Confirm yearly.
  • Region filters (East Bay commute, Bay Area, etc.) are preference tools, not admissions rules.

What this site is / is not

  • Is: a planning explorer for CA high school → college paths, with medicine as the default lens.
  • Is not: an official UC/CSU decision tool, a guarantee of admission, or visa / immigration advice.
  • Always confirm deadlines, majors, and residency on campus and system sites.

Sources (planning)

  • UC Dual Admission — official Dual Admission page

  • UC counselor materials (e.g. mid-25/75 GPA + admit rates for CA residents) — medium confidence when mapped to the slider scale

Confidence key

  • High: Official application windows, TAG campus list, Berkeley/UCLA/UCSD have no TAG.
  • Medium: GPA band mapping, TAG floors, transfer mid-50s, some major exclusions.
  • Low: Private admit odds, long-range job titles under AI, international seat chances.

How to use this (Gen Z version)

Money is a scoreboard for options — sleep, family, debt payoff, choosing your city. It’s not your whole story.
  • Pick a lane you’d still care about at 35, then notice what that lane usually pays.
  • High pay usually means longer training + harder residency matches (surgery, etc.) — not a cheat code.
  • CA dollars ≠ CA lifestyle automatically — rent and taxes hit hard in coastal metros.
  • Primary care still builds real lives — especially if you want community impact and sane hours.

Turn numbers into a work plan

  1. Circle 2–3 target lanes (ex: ortho vs family med vs PA vs clinic ops).
  2. Map the training years — undergrad → (MCAT) → school → residency/grad program.
  3. List skills that unlock that lane — GPA floor, MCAT band, clinical hours, ops projects.
  4. Protect energy — high-pay lanes punish burnout before they pay.

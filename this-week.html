Senior fall 2026 checklist

Focus list for California seniors aiming at UC / CSU this cycle (fall 2027 entry). Medicine path notes included.

Key windows: UC and CSU applications file Oct 1 – Nov 30, 2026. FAFSA / CA Dream Act open around Oct 1 (confirm each year). Do not try to file TAG this September — TAG is for CCC juniors later.

Do this week

Medicine path (chem / bio)

  • Stay on a biology + chemistry sequence this year. Add physics if your schedule allows.
  • Keep math moving (pre-calc or calculus if ready).
  • Start or continue one clinical exposure contact (hospital volunteer, clinic, EMT, hospice) — quality over title.
  • When you build a college list, note which campuses have strong bio / biochem paths for later MCAT or PA/NP routes.

UC PIQ quick tips

  • Answer with concrete scenes, not slogans.
  • Show growth: what you did, what you learned, what you do differently now.
  • Medicine interest is fine — focus on service, curiosity, and grit, not “I want to save lives” alone.

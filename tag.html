TAG & Dual Admission

Plain-language guide for California UC transfer paths. Written for grade 10 reading level.

If you are a high school senior this September: TAG is not for you right now. TAG is filed in September of your community college junior year. Keep TAG as a backup plan if you start at a CCC later.

What is TAG?

TAG means Transfer Admission Guarantee. It is a UC promise for eligible California Community College (CCC) students who meet GPA, course, and major rules. You pick one TAG campus and file in September.

TAG is part of the UC transfer system. It is separate from your UC freshman application this fall.

Six TAG campuses

Davis Irvine Merced Riverside Santa Barbara Santa Cruz
UC Davis — Strong bio/health options; some business/CS majors may be excluded from TAG.
UC Irvine — Popular; watch exclusions (e.g. some arts, nursing, and ICS majors).
UC Merced — Growing campus; often more TAG flexibility.
UC Riverside — Broad TAG majors; verify Art Studio and impacted lists yearly.
UC Santa Barbara — Engineering and some arts often excluded from TAG.
UC Santa Cruz — Generally open with screening; check major requirements.

No TAG at these UCs

These campuses do not offer TAG. You can still transfer there through the regular UC transfer process:

  • UC Berkeley
  • UCLA
  • UC San Diego

Dual Admission / Pathways+

After some freshman denials, UC may invite you to a California Community College pathway. That path can lead back to UC later. Pathways+ uses TAG-style mechanics for eligible students.

If an offer letter mentions Dual Admission, ask your counselor what campus and major it covers — and what GPA and courses you must keep.

Medicine path tip

If you want medicine later, look for TAG-eligible bio or health majors at Davis, Irvine, Merced, Riverside, UCSB, or UCSC. Always confirm major prep on ASSIST and the TAG matrix for your year.

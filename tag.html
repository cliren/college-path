TAG FAQ: CCC → UC Transfer Admission Guarantee

TAG (Transfer Admission Guarantee) is a UC program for eligible California Community College (CCC) students. If you meet campus GPA, unit, course, and major rules, one TAG campus guarantees your admission for that major.

You file TAG once, for one campus, through UC TAP in September. You still must submit the regular UC application in October–November for the same major.

TAG is a backup path for students already on (or planning) a CCC → UC transfer track — not a freshman tool.

HS seniors do NOT file TAG this September TAG is for CCC juniors (typically Sept 1–30 of the year before transfer). High school seniors applying as freshmen this fall should ignore the TAG filing window and use the UC freshman application instead.

Frequently asked questions

1. What is TAG?

Transfer Admission Guarantee is a contract-style promise from participating UC campuses to CCC students who meet published requirements (GPA, units, English/math pattern, major prep, and deadlines).

You choose one TAG campus. Meet every condition and that campus guarantees admission to the TAG major. Miss a condition and you still may be reviewed for regular transfer admission — but the guarantee is gone.

TAG is not binding: if you are admitted via TAG, you can still enroll elsewhere.

2. Which UCs offer TAG?

TAG campuses: UC Davis, UC Irvine, UC Merced, UC Riverside, UC Santa Barbara, UC Santa Cruz.

No TAG: UC Berkeley, UCLA, and UC San Diego. You can still transfer there through the regular UC transfer process (competitive review, no guarantee).

Campus cards and official pages are listed below.

3. When do I apply?
  • TAG via UC TAP: September 1–30 (year before you transfer).
  • UC application: October 1–November 30 for the same fall transfer term.
  • Majors must match: List the same major on TAG and on the UC app (usually as first choice). Changing the major after TAG can void the guarantee.
  • Many campuses also require a Transfer Academic Update (TAU) in January — confirm on the campus page.

4. What are the shared unit / GPA basics?

Exact floors differ by campus and major. Shared planning basics (confirm on the official matrix):

  • About 30 UC-transferable semester units completed by TAG submission (often by end of prior summer).
  • 60 UC-transferable semester units by the end of the spring term before transfer (with CCC unit / last-term rules).
  • English + math pattern: typically one English composition and one UC-transferable math by end of fall of the TAG year; a second English often by spring — campus wording varies.
  • Campus-specific minimum GPAs (often overall and/or major-prep) apply — some are higher for selective majors.

Use the official TAG matrix PDF for fall 2027 / spring 2028 planning — do not rely on memory or third-party summaries.

5. Can I TAG for any major?

No. Each campus excludes some majors (and sometimes entire colleges). Exclusions change by year.

Brief examples — confirm on the campus page + matrix before you file:

  • UC Davis: Business, Computer Science, Data Science, and other selective majors are often not TAG-eligible.
  • UC Irvine: commonly excludes Art, Business Administration, Dance, Music, Nursing, and all ICS majors.
  • UC Santa Barbara: excludes College of Creative Studies, College of Engineering, and some audition/portfolio arts majors.
  • Others: Merced, Riverside, and Santa Cruz also screen or exclude majors — check each year.

6. What is Dual Admission / Pathways+?

After some UC freshman outcomes, UC may invite students into a Dual Admission path that starts at a California Community College and can lead back to UC (often with TAG-style mechanics under Pathways+).

If an offer letter mentions Dual Admission or Pathways+, ask a counselor which campus/major it covers and which GPA and course conditions you must keep.

Official Dual Admission page (UC)

7. How does ASSIST fit?

ASSIST shows official articulation: which CCC courses count as major preparation for a specific UC campus and major.

TAG guarantees admission only when you complete the required major prep (and GPA) for that campus. Build your course plan on ASSIST, then verify TAG conditions on the campus page and matrix.

ASSIST.org — major preparation

8. What should a current HS senior do instead?
  • Do not open UC TAP to file TAG this September — that window is for CCC juniors transferring next fall.
  • File the UC freshman application Oct 1–Nov 30 if UC is a target.
  • Keep CCC → UC as a backup: strong A–G courses now, then a clear transfer plan (ASSIST major prep + TAG later) if you enroll at a community college.
  • If you receive a Dual Admission / Pathways+ invite after freshman decisions, read it carefully with a counselor.

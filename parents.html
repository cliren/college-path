For parents

This site helps students and parents plan California college paths together — packages, GPA levers, and realistic school lists. It is a planning tool, not an official admissions decision.

Student + parent CA default Fall 2027 entry

What this tool is for

  • Explore career packages (Medicine, Business, Engineering, CS, and more) with your student.
  • Move simple levers — GPA, start point, region, “need a likely seat” — and watch Likely / Target / Stretch lists update.
  • Open school cards for plain-language fit notes. Confirm anything that matters on the campus site.
  • California public paths (UC, CSU, CCC) are the default. Use the CA | US toggle on the home page if you want a wider U.S. framing.

This cycle — key dates

UC and CSU applications: file Oct 1 – Nov 30 (for fall 2027 entry).
FAFSA / CA Dream Act: open from about Oct 1 — confirm each year on the federal and CSAC sites.
No dollar amounts are shown in this tool on purpose; talk numbers with your counselor and aid offices.

CCC → TAG is normal in California

Starting at a California Community College and transferring with a TAG (Transfer Admission Guarantee) is a common, intentional path — not a failure path. Many strong students use it to build GPA, finish major prep, and lock a UC seat.

How to use the home page with your student

  1. Pick a package that matches interest (start with Medicine if that is the goal; you can switch later).
  2. Set the levers together — honest GPA, freshman vs CCC start, region focus, and whether a likely seat matters this cycle.
  3. Read the three stacks — Likely, Target, Stretch — and talk about one backup that does not need a perfect GPA year.
  4. Open a few school cards — note TAG flags, major caveats, and anything to verify with a counselor.
  5. Skim This week for a short senior-fall checklist you can work through at home.

What to ask a counselor this week

  • Are we on track for A–G (or CCC GE + major prep)? Any gaps to fix this term?
  • Which UC / CSU campuses and majors fit this GPA and course plan?
  • If CCC → transfer is on the table, which local colleges feed our intended major well (ASSIST.org)?
  • What is the school’s timeline for letters, transcripts, and FAFSA / Dream Act support?
  • For medicine-leaning students: bio + chem sequence, and one solid clinical or service contact.
